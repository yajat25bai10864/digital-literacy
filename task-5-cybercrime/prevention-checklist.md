# Stay Safe Online – Checklist for College Students
- Use strong, unique passwords for each account and enable 2-factor authentication (2FA).
- Never share your OTP, PIN, or passwords with anyone—even if they claim to be from a bank.
- Always verify UPI collect requests before approving; remember, receiving money does not require approval.
- Avoid clicking on unknown links in emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages.
- Install apps only from trusted sources like official app stores.
- Keep your devices updated with the latest security patches and antivirus software.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking or financial transactions.
- Regularly check your bank statements and UPI transaction history for suspicious activity.
- Adjust privacy settings on social media to limit who can see your personal information.
- Log out from shared or public devices after use.
